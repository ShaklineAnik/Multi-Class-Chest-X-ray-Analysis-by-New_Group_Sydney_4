Methods · system architecture

How CXR-Net reaches its prediction.

A short description of the pipeline, the model, and the explanation module that sits on top of the EfficientNetV2-S backbone.

§ A / PIPELINE

End-to-end stages

  1. Upload chest radiograph (PNG / JPG / BMP / WEBP).
  2. Convert to grayscale and resize to 224 × 224.
  3. Forward pass through EfficientNetV2-S backbone.
  4. Softmax over four class logits → probability vector.
  5. Grad-CAM over the final convolutional block → saliency overlay.
  6. Rationale module produces a natural-language explanation.

§ C / NOTES

Scope & limitations

The model was trained on a curated chest X-ray dataset spanning COVID-19, lung opacity, normal, and viral pneumonia classes. Off-distribution images (lateral views, pediatric studies, post-operative findings, foreign bodies) are out of scope and predictions on such inputs should not be trusted.

Research demonstration only · not a medical device · not a substitute for radiological review.
